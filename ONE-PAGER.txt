=========================================
PROBLEM
=========================================
Smallholder farmers in Rwanda lose 30-60% of crops to preventable diseases.
Extension officers are outnumbered 1:10,000. Most farmers rely on visual
inspection alone with no access to expert advice.

=========================================
SOLUTION
=========================================
A mobile-friendly AI assistant that:
1. Identifies crop diseases from a photo (Gemini Vision)
2. Provides treatment plans with locally available materials
3. Tracks recovery through photo check-ins with AI progress analysis
4. Works in English, Kinyarwanda, Swahili, and French
5. Reads treatments aloud for farmers with limited literacy

=========================================
HOW IT WORKS
=========================================
Farmer uploads photo → Gemini Vision detects disease
    → AI generates treatment plan with daily tasks
    → Farmer uploads follow-up photos at each stage
    → AI compares before/after → reports improving/stable/worsening
    → Dashboard tracks recovery across all plants

=========================================
TECH STACK
=========================================
Frontend:  Streamlit + Bootstrap Icons + dark theme
AI:        Google Gemini (vision + text)
Voice:    gTTS with speed control (0.75x - 1.5x)
Database: SQLite with Pandas
Demo:     Pre-loaded disease samples + cached audio

=========================================
KEY FEATURES
=========================================
• Disease detection from photo (real or demo)
• Smart Treatment Plan with staged recovery checklist
• AI progress tracking (before/after photo comparison)
• Voice playback in farmer's language
• Multi-language support (EN, RW, SW, FR)
• Treatment dashboard with recovery metrics
• Chat-style check-in for each plant
• Keep Healthy checklist for disease prevention
• Full assessment history and ledger

=========================================
ETHICS & BONUSES
=========================================
✓ Human-in-the-loop (AI advises, farmer decides)
✓ Evaluation metrics (confidence score, recovery rate)
✓ Personalization (resources, season, crop, location)
✓ Accessible (voice, dark theme, large buttons)
✓ Privacy-first (local SQLite, no cloud storage)

=========================================
BUSINESS MODEL / DEPLOYMENT
=========================================
Phase 1: Free pilot with 5 cooperatives in Rwanda
Phase 2: Freemium — free diagnosis, paid premium plans
Phase 3: Partner with Ministry of Agriculture for national rollout
Cost: $0 software. Runs on any laptop or phone browser.

=========================================
WHY THIS WINS
=========================================
• Real problem affecting millions of farmers
• Works offline with demo data (no internet? no problem)
• Voice support for low-literacy users
• Track record: Gemini API + Streamlit = reliable demo
• Scalable: one laptop serves an entire village
