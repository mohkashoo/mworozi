=== AGRISCOPE AI — Smart Farming Assistant ===
Frontiers GenAI Hackathon 2026 | Track 01: Agriculture & BioSystems
3 Crops: Cassava, Maize, Yam | Region: Rwanda / East Africa

=========================================
PROBLEM
=========================================
Smallholder farmers in Rwanda lose 30-50% of their crops to preventable
diseases and pests. They lack: timely scouting plans, climate-driven
growing advice, and direct market access. Extension officers are
outnumbered — farmers make decisions blind.

=========================================
SOLUTION — 3 Pillars
=========================================

PILLAR 1 — Smart Scouting
  Weekly scout plan: when, where, and what to look for
  5 spots x 4 plants = 20 sampling points, edges prioritized
  Gemini Vision photo check-in with instant AI diagnosis
  Low confidence → "Human Verification Recommended" flag
  Neighbor early warning alerts for nearby disease outbreaks
  4-step recovery follow plan with photo tracking

PILLAR 2 — Climate Growing Calendar
  Personalized season calendar: land prep → planting → weeding
    → fertilizer timing → harvest window
  Based on Open-Meteo free weather API (no key needed)
  Budget-constrained: stays within farmer's RWF budget
  Real-time adaptation: "delay planting 5 days — rains late"

PILLAR 3 — Market Connect
  Harvest estimate based on plot size + crop type
  Farmgate vs market price comparison with sell/hold advice
  5 seeded buyers per crop with contacts + WhatsApp messages
  AI-generated WhatsApp outreach in farmer's language
  Quality standards + negotiation tips
