=== MWOROZI — Presentation Slide Plan ===
3-minute demo + 2-minute Q&A | Track 01: Agriculture & BioSystems

=========================================
SLIDE 1 — TITLE (10 seconds)
=========================================
MWOROZI
AI Crop Health Assistant for East African Farmers
Frontiers GenAI Hackathon 2026 | Track 01: Agriculture
Team: [Names]

Speaker: "Mworozi means farmer in Kinyarwanda. We built an AI tool that
helps farmers detect crop diseases, get treatment plans, and connect
to buyers — all in their own language."

=========================================
SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM (25 seconds)
=========================================
Title: 30-50% of Harvests Lost to Preventable Diseases

Bullet points:
- 1 extension officer per 10,000 farmers in Rwanda
- Farmers rely on visual inspection alone
- Information that exists (FAO, IITA, CGIAR) never reaches the field
- No way to track treatment progress or access markets

Speaker: "Smallholder farmers lose up to half their crops every season
to diseases they could treat. Extension officers are outnumbered.
Farmers make decisions blind."

=========================================
SLIDE 3 — THE SOLUTION (25 seconds)
=========================================
Title: AI-Powered Crop Health — Diagnosis to Market

3 core capabilities:
1. Snap a photo → Gemini identifies disease + treatment plan
2. Smart recovery checklist with AI progress tracking
3. Market prices, buyer contacts, WhatsApp outreach

Speaker: "Three steps: snap photo, get diagnosis with treatment plan,
access buyers. All in Kinyarwanda, Swahili, French, or English."

=========================================
SLIDE 4 — DEMO WALKTHROUGH (30 seconds)
=========================================
Title: Live Demo — See It Work

Flow on screen:
1. Upload/take crop photo → click Analyze
2. Diagnosis appears: disease, severity, treatment, prevention
3. Share via WhatsApp (translated to Swahili)
4. Open recovery checklist → Day 1 to Day 14 tasks
5. Switch to Market tab → see buyers, prices, WhatsApp message

Speaker: "Let me show you. I'll take a photo of this maize leaf.
Gemini detects Northern Corn Leaf Blight. Treatment appears.
I tap WhatsApp — the full report opens in Swahili ready to send."

=========================================
SLIDE 5 — TECH & ARCHITECTURE (20 seconds)
=========================================
Title: How It Works Under the Hood

Diagram: Photo → Gemini Vision → Disease + Treatment
                      ↓
            Recovery Plan (4-stage checklist)
                      ↓
            Market Connect (buyers + prices + WhatsApp)
                      ↓
            Languages: EN / RW / SW / FR

Tech: Streamlit, Gemini 3.6-flash, SQLite, Open-Meteo, WhatsApp Web

Speaker: "Gemini Vision analyzes the photo. The treatment plan is
auto-generated. Market prices come from local data. All shared
via free WhatsApp and SMS — no API keys, no paid services."

=========================================

=========================================

- Every recommendation cited: FAO, IITA, CGIAR

Speaker: "Farmers pay nothing. The app works on any phone.
We're ready to pilot with 10 cooperatives tomorrow."
